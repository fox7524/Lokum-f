# Seed notes for key Victor Hugo texts

## Les Misérables
Social, legal, and spiritual epic. Central retrieval themes: poverty, redemption, surveillance, revolt, convents, sewers, moral transformation, grace versus law.

## Notre-Dame de Paris
Historical novel where architecture functions almost like a character. Central retrieval themes: Gothic heritage, desire, deformity, crowd spectacle, fatality, print versus stone.

## Les Contemplations
Essential for Hugo's elegiac and metaphysical voice, especially after Léopoldine's death. Central retrieval themes: grief, memory, soul, nature, afterlife.

## Les Châtiments
Satirical and prophetic anti-imperial verse directed against Napoleon III. Central retrieval themes: exile, denunciation, civic anger, invective, national shame.

## La Légende des siècles
Pan-historical poetic project. Central retrieval themes: civilization, human destiny, mythic scale, moral progress across epochs.

## Histoire d'un crime / Napoléon le Petit
Best sources for Hugo the political witness. Central retrieval themes: coup d'état, legitimacy, tyranny, republican memory, witness testimony.

## Correspondance / letters
Best source for Hugo's everyday voice, personal relations, patronage, travel remarks, and informal moral tone.
