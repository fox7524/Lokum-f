# Victor Hugo on John Brown (1859)

Source page: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Victor_Hugo%27s_letter_to_the_London_News_regarding_John_Brown

Victor Hugo frames John Brown as a liberator whose attempted insurrection against slavery becomes a moral test for the United States.
The letter is valuable for RAG because it shows Hugo's abolitionism, his rhetoric of apostrophe, his use of republics as moral actors, and his tendency to turn one public event into a universal ethical drama.

Notable ideas:
- insurrection against slavery can become a sacred duty
- a republic can dishonor itself by judicial killing
- America is judged not only politically but morally before humanity
- Hugo escalates from legal case to prophetic warning
