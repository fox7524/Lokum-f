# In Defense of His Son (1851)

Source page: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_World%27s_Famous_Orations/Volume_7/In_Defense_of_His_Son

Hugo uses his son's prosecution as a platform to restate his lifelong opposition to capital punishment. The speech is useful because it shows Hugo's courtroom oratory, his fusion of legal argument and moral theatre, and his insistence that critique of unjust law is a civic duty.

Notable ideas:
- criticism of penal law is a legitimate public right
- the execution scene becomes an indictment of the state itself
- Hugo's anti-death-penalty voice is inseparable from his political ethics
